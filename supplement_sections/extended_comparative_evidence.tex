\section{Extended Comparative Evidence}

This section assembles structured evidence for cross-domain comparison under the same five-field frame. It states the synthesis rules explicitly rather than reproducing a paper inventory mechanically.

\subsection{Cross-Domain Reading Key}

The compact cross-domain visual and reading-key table shows how the same governance seams recur under different state lifetimes. The point is not to re-rank domains.

The comparison rule is straightforward: visibility and ownership dominate streaming, movement and quality boundaries dominate hardware-conscious execution, short-lived lifecycle control dominates serving, and maintenance debt dominates retrieval and retention.

\section{Scope and Relation to the Main Paper}

The supplement adds supporting evidence, longer comparative paths, and structured matrices without changing the survey boundary. The added material falls into four classes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item broader literature landscape material that helps locate the survey in neighboring systems traditions;
\item longer mechanism-level comparative evidence for cross-domain comparison under a shared analytical frame;
\item worked examples and case-study style walkthroughs that make propagation arguments concrete;
\item structured matrices that keep comparisons explicit without interrupting the core narrative.
\end{enumerate}

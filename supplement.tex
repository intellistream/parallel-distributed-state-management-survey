\section{Scope and Relation to the Main Paper}

This supplement collects supporting evidence, longer comparative paths, and structured matrices.

Its role is to make the survey's claims auditable without changing the survey boundary. The added material falls into four classes:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item broader literature landscape material that helps locate the survey in neighboring systems traditions;
\item longer mechanism-level comparative evidence that would otherwise turn the main paper back into a literature inventory;
\item worked examples and case-study style walkthroughs that make propagation arguments concrete;
\item structured matrices that support auditability but are not necessary for the main paper's argumentative spine.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{keybox}
This supplement is evidence infrastructure for the same survey argument. Material belongs here when it strengthens a claim but would consume disproportionate page budget in the core article.
\end{keybox}
